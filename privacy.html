Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-18

This is the short version: we collect as little as we can, we don't sell anything, and the only third parties who get your data are the ones we need to deliver the service.

What we collect

  • First name and date of birth — you type these to generate your Signature. Stored in your browser (localStorage) and, for paying members, in metadata on your Stripe subscription so you can sign in from a new device.
  • Email — collected at checkout. Used to sign you in and to send a renewal warning the day before each charge. Stored in Stripe and in our email sender (Resend) for the purpose of sending sign-in codes.
  • Payment information — collected by Stripe directly. We never see your card number; we only receive a token and the last four digits.
  • Basic server logs — IP address, timestamp, and endpoint when you call our API. Used only for security/debugging. Auto-deleted after 30 days.

What we don't collect

No tracking pixels, no cross-site cookies, no third-party analytics, no advertising IDs, no behavioral fingerprinting. We don't have an ad business.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe — to process payments. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • Resend — to send your sign-in code emails. See Resend's privacy policy.
  • Vercel — our hosting provider. See Vercel's privacy policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with anyone else. We don't have a data-broker relationship of any kind.

How long we keep it

While your subscription is active. If you cancel, your Stripe customer record persists for tax/legal compliance (typically 7 years in the US). Your sign-in metadata is removed from active systems within 30 days of cancellation; ask us if you want it removed sooner.

Your rights

Email hello@kismet.cards to:

  • See what we have about you
  • Correct or update it
  • Delete it (we'll cancel any active subscription and remove your data within 30 days)
  • Object to processing

If you're in the EU/UK or California, you have specific rights under GDPR/CCPA — same email, same handling.

Cookies

We do not set tracking cookies. We use localStorage on your device to remember your sign-in and reading state. You can clear it any time from your browser settings.

Children

Kismet is not for anyone under 16. We don't knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe we have, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we make material changes, we'll email you at least 14 days before they take effect.
