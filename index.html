Astrology-adjacent. We use birth data, but the Signature is its own system — built to surface patterns that don't fit neatly into twelve boxes. It's the part of horoscopes that actually felt like it knew you.

How is it free?

Your first reading and your Signature card are free, always. The daily reading, compatibility, and the Year Ahead live behind the membership — that's how we keep the lights on and the servers warm.

How much is membership?

Does my name actually matter?

Yes. The letters get translated into a number sequence; the sequence gets folded against your birth date. Same name + same birthday = same Signature, every time. That's the test — try it.
